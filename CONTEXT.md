# Babyroo

Babyroo helps parents choose places and events for outings with young children.

## Language

**Coverage area**:
A named geographic area covered by the public crowding data source. A venue belongs to a coverage area only after its location has been verified to lie inside the official boundary.

**Surrounding-area crowding**:
The source's estimated crowding level for a coverage area at an observation time. It does not describe the crowding inside an individual venue.
_Avoid_: Venue crowding

**Crowding observation**:
A source-reported crowding level for one coverage area and boundary version at a specific observation time. Fetching it again does not create a new observation; a source correction revises the same observation.

**Hourly crowding history**:
A sequence of hourly crowding levels, each represented by the latest available observation within that hour. Hours without observations are gaps.

**Crowding comparison target**:
A requested date and time compared with the same time one through seven days earlier. Its reading is the latest observation at or before the target within the same Korea-time hour; absent observations remain unavailable and actual observation times are shown.

**Historical crowding reference**:
An observed crowding level from the same weekday last week at the target hour, used as context when considering an outing. It is a dated historical reading, not a prediction of future conditions.
_Avoid_: Forecast, predicted crowding

**Area unlock**:
Access to current crowding and the rolling seven-day hourly history for one coverage area for 24 hours from purchase. The same access applies to all Babyroo venues mapped to that area.

**Credit**:
Babyroo's shared unit for purchasing recommendations and area unlocks.
_Avoid_: Token
