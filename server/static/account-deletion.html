Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 15, 2026

BabyRoo supports deletion of Google-linked BabyRoo accounts and related user data. This page explains how users can delete their account and what data is removed.

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your BabyRoo account, sign in to BabyRoo with the Google account you used for BabyRoo and use the account deletion option in the app when available.

BabyRoo also provides an authenticated deletion API for the signed-in user:

  • DELETE /api/users/me

This API requires the BabyRoo access token for the account being deleted. It cannot be used to delete another user's account.

Data Deleted

When your BabyRoo account is deleted, BabyRoo deletes:

  • Your BabyRoo user profile linked to Google Sign-In.
  • Your display name and BabyRoo account identifier.
  • Your home region, selected localities, and home address data.
  • Your child profiles saved in BabyRoo.
  • Your saved events and recommendation sessions.
  • Your recommendation-credit account and credit ledger records.
  • Your Google Play purchase records stored by BabyRoo.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, or platform compliance reasons. This may include limited server logs, aggregate service records, or records required to verify past Google Play purchases.

Google Account Data

Deleting your BabyRoo account deletes the BabyRoo data linked to your Google Sign-In account. It does not delete your Google account or data held directly by Google. You can manage your Google account data through Google's own account settings.

Need Help?

If you cannot access the app or need help deleting your account, please contact us at the developer support email listed on BabyRoo's Google Play Store listing.
