Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BabyRoo collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the BabyRoo mobile application and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BabyRoo, we may collect:

  • Account information from Google Sign-In, such as your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile photo.
  • Profile and preference information you provide, such as your home region, selected localities, saved events, and recommendation preferences.
  • Child profile information you provide, such as nickname, birth date, and gender, to personalize recommendations.
  • Address search information selected through the in-app address search feature.
  • Purchase information related to recommendation credits, such as Google Play product ID, purchase token, order ID, purchase state, and credit ledger records.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service, such as API requests, authentication tokens, and server logs.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide login and account features.
  • Show family-friendly places, events, and recommendations.
  • Save user preferences, children profiles, and saved events.
  • Process and verify Google Play in-app purchases.
  • Generate personalized recommendations.
  • Maintain, debug, secure, and improve BabyRoo.

Third-Party Services

BabyRoo uses third-party services to provide core app functionality. These services may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

  • Google Sign-In, for authentication.
  • Google Play Billing, for in-app purchases.
  • Google Play Developer API, for purchase verification.
  • Kakao Postcode, for address search.
  • OpenAI API, for recommendation generation when enabled.
  • Vercel, for hosting BabyRoo backend services.
  • Neon PostgreSQL, for database hosting.
  • Vercel Blob, for storing event images managed by BabyRoo admins.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only when needed to operate BabyRoo, comply with law, protect users and the service, or use the third-party services listed above.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide BabyRoo, maintain purchase and credit records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business reasons.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information, including encrypted network communication where applicable. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

BabyRoo is designed for parents and guardians who are looking for family-friendly outings. BabyRoo is not intended for children to use without parent or guardian involvement. Parents or guardians may provide child profile information to receive age-appropriate recommendations.

International Processing

Information may be processed and stored in countries other than your country of residence, depending on the locations of BabyRoo and its service providers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us at the developer support email listed on BabyRoo's Google Play Store listing.
